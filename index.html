School identity

A minimal school with a clear scientific thread

This edition focuses on a precise arc: how probability provides the language of uncertainty, how information theory gives principled objectives, and how both can be used to build useful models.

P

Probability

The mathematical language of uncertainty and fluctuations.

  • Random variables
  • Law of large numbers
  • Central limit theorem
  • Concentration and large deviations
  • Examples in open quantum systems
I

Information

The quantities that tell us what should be preserved, compressed, or predicted.

  • Shannon entropy and converse ideas
  • Maximum Entropy principle
  • InfoMax
  • PredVIB
  • Maximum Relevance principle
M

Models

The constructive step: turning principles into concrete rules, algorithms, and code.

  • Data-driven examples
  • Code notebooks for students
  • GitHub repository for reproducibility
  • Minimal but rigorous derivations
  • Applications across complex systems
